# Results + life stage + thank-you video concept

Review: https://nithish6541.github.io/house-of-tails-scroll-quiz-mock/design-concepts/results-video/

Combined end-of-quiz design review using fictional dog/cat profiles. Select Dog,
Cat, or Dog & Cat at the top. Cat examples show Kitten (8 months), Young adult
(3 years), Mature adult (8 years) and Senior (12 years) alongside the thank-you
video and a separate supplement-result placeholder. Dog life-stage calculation
is not included yet. Nothing is submitted or saved, and there is no supplement
recommendation calculation or physiological-age estimate.

Four selectable example tiles sit within the results card, immediately above
life stage. The selected tile uses the wine highlight and a checkmark. The
controls support keyboard activation and stay still while the result changes;
switching examples does not interrupt a playing video. Reduced motion is respected.

Feline age bands come from the 2021 AAHA/AAFP guidelines linked on the page.
Month-level implementation: Kitten 0–11 months, Young adult 12–83, Mature adult
84–120, Senior 121 onward. Result wording and boundary interpretation are draft
implementation details; this is not a personalised health or lifespan assessment.
The former standalone age-entry calculator was removed rather than published.

The supplied reference clip demonstrates video placement and playback only; it
is not either final species-specific thank-you message. Both species states use
the same labelled reference. Final dog/cat edits and accurate captions are still
needed before production integration. The video is user-initiated, not autoplay
or a requirement for viewing results.

This directory is a self-contained export of the approved local concept. Brand
artwork is client-supplied. Bodoni Moda fonts are distributed with their SIL Open
Font License in `bodoni-OFL.txt`. No live quiz settings or customer records are
included. The existing scroll-quiz mock is unchanged.
